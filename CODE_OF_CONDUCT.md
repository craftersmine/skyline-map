# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience level, background or language.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexual content.
- Don't share other people's private information.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**, which only the maintainer can read) and say it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
